# Design System - Nordic Forest & Tactile Studio

<!-- impeccable:design-schema 1 -->

## Visual Identity

A tactile, organic Finnish puzzle studio identity anchored in rich **Nordic Pine Green** (`#064e3b`), **Emerald** (`#047857`), **Soft Sage** (`#e6f4ed`), and **Warm Oatmeal Paper** (`#fbfaf6`).

## Color System

- **Background**: `#fbfaf6` (Warm Oatmeal paper ground with subtle dot grid texture)
- **Primary Brand / Banner**: `#064e3b` (Deep Finnish Pine Green)
- **Primary Action**: `#064e3b` (Pine Green button, hovering to `#047857`)
- **Accent / Badges**: `#047857` (Rich Emerald for piece count badges)
- **Sage Highlights**: `#e6f4ed` (Soft Sage containers & focus states)
- **Text Main**: `#0f291e` (Deep Forest Charcoal)
- **Text Muted**: `#4a6b5d` (Muted Pine Leaf)
- **Borders**: `#e2ede7` (Organic Sage Border)

## Component Language

- **Cards**: Warm white paper cards (`bg-white border-[#e2ede7]`) with soft shadow elevation (`shadow-[#064e3b]/8`).
- **Badges**: Deep forest store badges (`bg-[#0f291e]/90 text-emerald-100`) and emerald piece count badges (`bg-[#047857] text-emerald-50`).
- **Buttons**: Tactile pine green buttons (`bg-[#064e3b]`) with crisp focus rings (`focus-visible:ring-[#047857]`).
- **Inputs & Selects**: Soft sage fields (`bg-[#f4f8f5] border-[#d2e6db]`) with deep forest text.
